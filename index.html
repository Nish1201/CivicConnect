How It Works

A Simple Path to Collaboration

CivicConnect turns local challenges into opportunities for shared problem-solving.

01 Step One

Describe a Local Challenge

A community member describes an everyday problem—water quality, storage loss, accessibility or similar—in plain words.

02 Step Two

Students Explore Solutions

University teams explore the challenge and propose practical approaches—engineering, design, research or process improvement.

03 Step Three

Partners Offer Support

Organisations and mentors contribute expertise, materials, testing access or funding so ideas can move toward implementation.
